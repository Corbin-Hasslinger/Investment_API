## Atlas Product Principles

** Version: 1.0
** Last Updated: August 2026

## Principles

1. Explainability Over Recommendation

Atlas should prioritize helping users understand investment opportunities rather than simply recommending what to buy or sell. Every insight should be accompanied by clear reasoning and supporting evidence whenever possible.

2. Evidence before AI

Artificial intelligence should interpret financial data, not replace it. AI-generated responses must be grounded in verifiable market data and should clearly distinguish factual information from generated explanations.

3. Current and Transparent Data

Investment decisions depend on timely information. Atlas should provide up-to-date market data whenever available and clearly indicate when data was last refreshed.

4. User-Controlled Analysis

Users define their own investing goals and screening criteria. Atlas should provide tools for analysis rather than enforcing a single investment philosophy or strategy.

5. Learn While Investing

Atlas is designed to educate as well as analyze. Whenever practical, the platform should help users understand financial concepts, portfolio metrics, and market behavior alongside the information it provides.

6. Build for Growth

Every major design decision should consider future expansion while avoiding unnecessary complexity in the current implementation. The MVP should establish a solid foundation without overengineering features that are not yet required.
