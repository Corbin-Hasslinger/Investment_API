## Atlas MVP Scope

** Version: 1.0
** Last Updated: August 2026

## Included

- User account creation and authentication.
- Multiple named investment portfolios.
- Manual portfolio creation and management.
- Investment position management.
- Live market data retrieval.
- Portfolio analytics and performance metrics.
- Company and stock research.
- AI-powered portfolio and investment explanations.
- Stock screening using customizable criteria.

## Out of Scope

- Brokerage account synchronization.
- Automatic portfolio updates.
- Trade execution.
- Portfolio optimization.
- Investment simulations.
- Options, cryptocurrency, and international markets.
- Notifications and alerts.
- Social and collaborative features.
