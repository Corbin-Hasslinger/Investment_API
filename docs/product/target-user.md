## Atlas Target User

** Version 1.0
** Last Updated: August 2026

## Primary User

The primary user of Atlas is a self-directed beginner investor who actively manages their own investments but lacks the experience, time, or resources to efficiently research companies, evaluate portfolio performance, and understand market conditions.

## User Characteristics

The primary Atlas user:

- Invests independently without a financial advisor.
- Owns or plans to build a long-term investment portfolio.
- Wants to better understand their current holdings.
- Is interested in learning about investing while using the platform.

## Future Audience

While the MVP focuses on beginner investors, Atlas is designed to expand its capabilities to support more experienced investors through advanced analytics, portfolio optimization, quantitative research tools, and additional market analysis features.
