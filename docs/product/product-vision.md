# Atlas Product Vision

## Vision Statement

Atlas is an AI-powered investment research platform that helps self-directed investors understand their portfolios, interpret market data, discover investment opportunities using customizable criteria, and make informed investment decisions through data-driven analysis.

Rather than acting as a brokerage or financial advisor, Atlas serves as an intelligent research assistant that combines financial data, portfolio analytics, and artificial intelligence into a single platform.

## Mission

Enable individual investors to make better-informed investment decisions by reducing the time and effort required to gather, analyze, and understand financial information.

Atlas emphasizes explanation over recommendation, empowering users to understand *why* an investment may or may not fit their goals instead of simply suggesting what to buy.

## Product Goals

Atlas aims to:

- Centralize investment research into a single platform
- Simplify complex financial information
- Provide transparent AI-powered explanations
- Analyze portfolio composition and risk
- Help users discover investment opportunities using customizable constraints
- Build a scalable backend capable of supporting future advanced investment tools

## Long-Term Vision

Atlas will evolve into a comprehensive investment platform capable of:

- advanced portfolio analytics
- quantitative investment screening
- portfolio optimization
- risk analysis
- AI-assisted research
- market monitoring
- investment simulations
- broker integrations
- personalized financial insights

Every feature should support the overarching mission of helping investors understand the market rather than replacing their judgment.
