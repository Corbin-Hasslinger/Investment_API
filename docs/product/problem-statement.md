## Atlas Problem Statement

** Version: 1.0
** Last Updated: August 2026

## Problem

Self-directed investors often rely on multiple disconnected sources to research companies, monitor market conditions, and evaluate their investment portfolios. As a result, they spend significant time gathering information and may struggle to interpret financial data or understand how individual investments contribute to their overall portfolio.

## Opportunity

Provide a centralized platform that combines portfolio management, market data, financial analysis, and AI-powered explanations into a single experience.
